Files: `fig3_combined.{png,pdf}` (7.16 in wide, panels a-d), each panel alone (`fig3a_slides`, `fig3b_tiles`, `fig3c_access`,

## Sources
- GigaPath: Xu et al., Nature 2024, 'A whole-slide foundation model for digital pathology from real-world data' (171,189 slides, 1,384,860,229 tiles, >30,000 patients, 28 centers).
- H-Optimus-0: Hugging Face model card (>500,000 H&E slides); slide count also in GenBio-PathFM Table B9. '4,000+ clinical centers' and 'several hundred million tiles' appear only in press coverage.
- GenBio-PathFM: bioRxiv 2026.03.17.712534, Tables B8 (HistAI 112k/253M, TCGA 32k/73M, GTEx 25k/56M, REG 8k/18M; total 177k slides / 400M tiles) and B9.
- UNI2-h: model card (>200 M tiles, >350,000 slides, Mass General Brigham; read via a search result, not opened directly) and GenBio-PathFM Table B9.
- Virchow2: arXiv 2408.00738 (3.1 M slides, 225,401 patients, 5x/10x/20x/40x, H&E + IHC).
- CONCH v1.5: model card (ViT-L/16 restored from UNI, 1.17 M image-caption pairs); UNI = >100 M patches from >100k slides (Kaiko paper), 100 K slides (GenBio-PathFM Table B9).
- Kaiko ViT-B/16: arXiv 2404.15217 (29 k TCGA slides, online patching, no fixed tile count).
- CTransPath: '15M tiles from 32K WSIs' as cited in the Virchow2 paper; the original paper was not opened.
- ResNet50: weights `timm/resnet50.tv_in1k` per the HEST repository; ImageNet-1k has about 1.28 M training images.
- ePathST-E / ePathST: project manifests (363 CPTAC slides, 564,312 patches; 291 training patients, 450,965 training patches).
- Params, relative size and avg. PCC: from the paper's HEST comparison table.
